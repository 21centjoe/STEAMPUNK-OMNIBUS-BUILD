II

The Traveler, The Auto Emulator, & The Cosmic Syndicate

Across both the Old and New Worlds, Dan the traveler planted civilization millennia ago, leaving encrypted mechanical relics. When a hippie kid stumbles into a cave and boots up the dormant auto emulator, a hidden layer of reptilian entities and the mysterious matron is exposed. Simultaneously, the exact same cosmic intelligence orchestrated the ancient Druids of the Old World and the monumental empires of the Aztecs and Mayans, overseen by celestial Arthurian dragons and cosmic knights.

III

The Pecos River Railroad & The Golden Codex

Through an accidental temporal rift, Isaac Newton’s lineage culminates in a railroad worker on the Pecos River frontier. Alongside Omar (from Asia Minor) and Taoist brothers from Hunan whose mother is a priestess, they decode holographic I Ching matrixes. When a telegram to San Francisco confirms the ancient prophecy, they uncover the strangest anomaly: every word and formula in the golden codex is written entirely in Latin and English.

IV

The Siege of Vinegaroon & The Sub-River Portal

Pandemic chaos erupts across Vinegaroon as unstable plasma dragons materialize from everywhere, drawn to the golden book. Evading the swarm into Indian territory and the Big Bend, the protagonist discovers a cavern hidden beneath the river's surface where a guide asks, "Are you here to see it?" ushering them into a shimmering dimensional gateway.

V

Magister Newton’s Light Theory & The Lesser Key

Magister Newton’s master architecture relies heavily on advanced light theory, prisms, and continuous gyroscopic stabilization. Fuming with relentless contempt for the probabilistic ambiguities of quantum mechanics, he forced string theory into absolute clockwork equations. Meanwhile, atmospheric scrubbers inhale industrial soot, exhaling pristine oxygen, while the mechanized Lesser Key of Solomon physically compels dimensional entities to maintain universal baseline stability.

Quark Fusion & Reluctant Quantum Mechanics

Newton maintained an intense, bitter contempt for quantum mechanics, grumbling endlessly about probability waves and wave-function collapses. VIII

The ultimate, mind-shattering revelation breaks across the control panels as the digital simulation parameters align: the rustic frontier towns, the Pecos railroad, the soaring Laputa islands, and the vast oceans are not planetary geography at all. Worse still, long-range sensor arrays confirm the terrifying truth: the starship is on an unalterable trajectory toward an inevitable, catastrophic stellar impact. The inhabitants are passengers in a colossal computer program who only now realize they must initiate emergency evacuation protocols before the final collision.
